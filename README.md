# MaalFlow

**Local Indian Inventory Management System.** A stock register for shops that works by
printing and scanning QR labels. It runs on one Windows computer, in English, हिंदी
and मराठी. Your stock data stays on your own computer.

## Download

**[Download the installer](https://github.com/vairagadeharshwardhan/maalflow/releases/latest/download/LocalIndianInventory-Setup.exe)**
(about 35 MB, for 64-bit Windows 10 or 11)

This link always gives the newest version.

## Install

1. Open the file you downloaded and press **Install**. No administrator password is
   needed.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run
   anyway**.

## Activate

The app needs a licence key from your supplier. You do this once.

1. Connect the computer to the internet. A phone hotspot is enough.
2. Open **Local Indian Inventory** from the Desktop. It shows **Activate this
   computer**.
3. Scan the QR code on your licence card with your QR scanner, or type the key, and
   press **Activate**.

From then on the app works without the internet.

| If it says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The internet could not be reached | Connect the computer to the internet and press **Activate** again |
| This key is not known | Check every letter and digit against your licence card. A key has no letters I, L, O or U |
| The licence has already been used, or its time for activating has passed | Ask your supplier to open your key again |
| The computer's date or time is wrong | Correct the Windows clock, then press **Activate** again |

## Updating

Download from the same link and install over the old version. Your stock data and
your licence are kept.

## The `licences` folder

It holds one small record for each licence key. A record can be read only with its
own key, so it tells nobody anything about any customer.
